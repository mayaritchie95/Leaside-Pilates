GYROTONIC®

Flowing, circular movement on specialized equipment to build mobility and move with freedom.

The Method

Mobility, decompression, and flow

The GYROTONIC® method uses specialized equipment and flowing, circular movements to encourage mobility, strength, coordination, and freedom of movement.

Unlike traditional linear exercise, movements travel through multiple planes, allowing the spine and joints to move naturally through a full range of motion. Each private session is adapted to your body, experience, and individual goals.

Who It's For

Who is GYROTONIC® for?

GYROTONIC® sessions can be a great choice if you want to:

Improve Mobility

Explore greater freedom and range of motion through the spine and joints.

Build Strength & Coordination

Develop strength through smooth, connected, three-dimensional movement.

Balance Your Training

Complement Pilates, strength training, running, dance, or other movement practices.

Move With More Ease

A personalized approach for anyone looking to feel more connected and comfortable in their body.

Your Session

Your GYROTONIC® session

Each session is one-on-one and tailored to you. Your instructor will guide you through movements using the GYROTONIC® Pulley Tower, adjusting the equipment and exercises based on your body, mobility and goals.

Experience GYROTONIC®

Whether you're completely new to the method or already familiar with GYROTONIC®, we'll help you get started with a private session tailored to you.
