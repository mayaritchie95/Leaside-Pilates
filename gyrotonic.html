The Method

Mobility, decompression, and flow

Gyrotonic® uses specialized equipment and circular, three-dimensional movement to decompress the spine, mobilize the joints, and build strength through a full range of motion.

Offered as private sessions in our dedicated space, it's a beautiful complement to Pilates — or a practice all its own.
