Private, Duo & Trio Pilates

Pilates designed around you

Private sessions are built entirely around you — your body, your goals, and your experience level. They're ideal if you're new to Pilates, returning to movement after an injury or surgery, or working toward a specific outcome.

We also work with prenatal and postnatal clients, as well as those looking to build strength, improve mobility and balance, or support their performance in sports and everyday activities.

Work in a fully equipped Pilates studio using the Reformer, Tower, Cadillac, Stability Chair and other specialized Pilates equipment, selected according to your individual needs and goals.
