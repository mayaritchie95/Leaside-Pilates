Private Pilates sessions

Fully individualized one-on-one, duo, and trio sessions in our dedicated private studio (Unit 109).

One-on-One

The fastest, most tailored path to results

Private sessions are built entirely around you — your body, your goals, and your experience level. They're ideal if you're new to Pilates, returning to movement after an injury or surgery, or working toward a specific outcome.

We work with prenatal and postnatal clients, and with people looking to improve performance in golf, tennis, pickleball, and other sports.
