General

Both build strength, mobility, coordination, and body awareness, but they approach movement differently.

Pilates focuses on controlled, precise movements with an emphasis on full-body strength, stability, and alignment. Exercises tend to be more linear and structured, performed on specialized equipment such as the Reformer, Tower, Cadillac, Stability Chair, and Ladder Barrel.

GYROTONIC® uses continuous, flowing, circular, three-dimensional movements, with more emphasis on spinal mobility, rotation, coordination, and creating freedom and space throughout the body. Sessions are performed on specialized GYROTONIC® equipment.

At Leaside Pilates, we offer private Pilates and private GYROTONIC® sessions, as well as group Pilates classes.

Pilates and GYROTONIC® are designed for every body and every fitness level. Whether you're new to exercise, curious to try something new, or already active and looking to complement your routine, both methods can be adapted to meet you where you are.

No previous Pilates experience is required to join our group classes. Our Level 1 class is designed specifically for beginners, including clients who have never used Pilates equipment before. Before your first class, your instructor will walk you through the equipment and provide guidance and modifications throughout so you feel comfortable and confident as you learn.

Grip socks are mandatory to help maintain a clean and hygienic studio environment. You're welcome to bring your own, or purchase a pair of Leaside Pilates grip socks at the studio for $12 — just ask your instructor when you arrive.

Our private studio is a shared space, with multiple clients having private sessions at the same time. To keep the environment calm for everyone, please put your phone on silent before entering. Our group classes are also a phone-free space — please keep your phone on silent and avoid using it during class.

There is a large free parking lot directly in front of our studio building. Additional parking is available at Longo's Plaza, about a 5-minute walk from the studio.

The closest bus stop is Industrial St at Laird Dr, served by TTC bus line 51. Lines 88A, 88B, and 156 also operate nearby. The studio is a 15-minute walk from Laird LRT station (Line 5).

We recommend joining one of our Prenatal Specialty Pilates classes, designed specifically for pregnancy — especially in your third trimester. You're also welcome to join a Level 1 group class, where your instructor can provide modifications as needed.

For a more personalized approach, private Pilates sessions are tailored to your individual needs, movement, and stage of pregnancy — a great option if you're experiencing discomfort such as lower back pain, or simply prefer more individual attention. Our experienced instructors are trained to work with prenatal and postnatal clients and can adapt sessions as your body and needs change.

We recommend consulting your healthcare provider before beginning or continuing Pilates during pregnancy, particularly if you have any concerns or are experiencing pain or other symptoms.

Packages & Subscriptions

Subscriptions are for clients who want a consistent practice at a lower per-session rate. Sessions are used within each monthly billing cycle, and the subscription renews automatically each month.

Packages offer more flexibility, giving you more time to use your sessions (up to 6 months) without a monthly commitment.

If you come regularly and want better value, choose a subscription. If you prefer flexibility, choose a package.

If you choose a subscription for private sessions, we recommend reserving a regular day and time each week. This keeps you consistent and ensures a time that works for you with your preferred instructor, as our private schedule can get busy. Your reserved session can always be rescheduled when needed, as long as at least 24 hours' notice is provided.

Intro packages must be used within 21 days of purchase. Discounted packages must be used within 4 months of purchase. All other packages must be used within 6 months of purchase.

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, with no minimum commitment period. We require 2 weeks' notice before the end of your current billing cycle. To request a cancellation, please email hello@leasidepilates.com.

Yes. We require 2 weeks' notice to place a subscription on hold. Please email hello@leasidepilates.com with the dates you'd like your hold to begin and end.

Bookings & Cancellations

Private sessions are booked directly with our studio. Please email hello@leasidepilates.com or text us at 647-550-9968.

We recommend downloading the Momence app to easily manage your sessions. Private sessions are most easily cancelled through the Momence app or by logging into your profile on our website.

Group classes can be booked or cancelled directly through the Momence app or by logging into your account on our website. Once you've purchased a package or subscription, simply choose your class and reserve your spot.

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy for all group classes and private, duo, and trio sessions. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are considered a late cancellation, and the session credit will be forfeited. This policy helps us manage the schedule efficiently and gives all clients the opportunity to book available spots.

If a class is full, we recommend adding yourself to the waitlist. If a spot opens up more than 1 hour before the class starts, you'll automatically be added and notified by email.

To join the waitlist you'll need an active package or subscription. If you don't have an active credit, the system will prompt you to purchase a single class credit; if you're not added to the class, your credit is returned. Note that the 24-hour cancellation policy applies once you're added to a class, even if that's less than 24 hours before it begins — so if you can no longer attend, please remove yourself from the waitlist to avoid losing your credit.

Specialty Classes

Our Specialty Pilates classes are designed for specific stages of life, movement needs, and fitness goals. They offer a more focused approach than our regular group classes, with programming tailored to the needs of each group. Led by experienced, knowledgeable instructors, each class provides targeted, purposeful instruction in a supportive small-group environment, with individual attention and modifications when needed.

Specialty classes run as a 6-week series, one class per week. When registering, you'll purchase the full 6-class series at $35 per class ($210 total), and you'll automatically be enrolled in all six classes.

Your registration reserves your spot for the entire program. If you can't attend one of the scheduled classes, that class credit is forfeited and can't be carried to another week. Past specialty classes have included Mommy & Baby, Prenatal, and Active Aging. Please check each class description on our website for details on what to expect and who it's designed for.
