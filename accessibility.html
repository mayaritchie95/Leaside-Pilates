Our commitment

Leaside Pilates is committed to making our website and studio accessible to people of all abilities, in line with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and WCAG 2.1 guidelines where reasonably achievable.

Website accessibility

We strive to follow best practices including keyboard navigation, sufficient colour contrast, descriptive text alternatives for images, and respecting reduced-motion preferences. We continue to review and improve accessibility over time.

Studio accessibility

For questions about physical access to the studio, or to request an accommodation for a class or session, please contact us in advance and we\'ll do our best to assist.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our website or at the studio, please let us know so we can address it. Email iva.mazar@gmail.com or call 647-550-9968.
