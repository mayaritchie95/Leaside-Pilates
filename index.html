Our Studio

More than a workout — a place to move better and feel confident.

Leaside Pilates is a modern, welcoming studio built around personalized, high-quality instruction. Across two dedicated spaces, we help you build strength, improve mobility, and move with ease.

Unit 108 is home to our small group classes; Unit 109 is reserved for private, duo, and trio sessions and Gyrotonic®. Whether you're brand new to Pilates or years into your practice, there's a place for you here.
