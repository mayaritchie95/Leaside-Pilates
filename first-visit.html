Getting Started

What is Pilates?

Pilates is a low-impact method that builds core strength, mobility, and control through precise, mindful movement — often on specialized equipment like the Reformer. It suits every level, from complete beginners to seasoned athletes, and is especially effective for building strength safely.

How to begin

New clients start with an intro offer for either group classes or private sessions. Not sure which is right for you? Private sessions are ideal if you\'re new, recovering from injury, or working toward a specific goal; group classes are a great, social way to build a consistent practice.

Good to Know

Studio policies

Arrival

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit so we can get you settled and answer any questions.

What to wear

Comfortable, fitted activewear you can move in. Grip socks are recommended for Pilates and Gyrotonic®.

Cancellations

We ask for advance notice to cancel or reschedule so we can offer the spot to someone else. Full cancellation window and policy details are confirmed at booking in Momence.

Booking & payment

All classes, sessions, and packages are booked and paid through Momence. You can create an account, purchase, and manage your schedule online.
