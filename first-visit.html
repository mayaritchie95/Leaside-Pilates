How to Begin

Find the right starting point

Private Sessions

Best for: Complete beginners who prefer one-on-one attention, clients with injuries or pain, prenatal or postnatal clients, those returning after surgery, or anyone with specific movement goals.

Your instructor will assess your needs and create sessions tailored specifically to you. Start with our 3-Session Private Intro Offer for $285.

To book: Email or call Leaside Pilates, fill out our contact form, or send us a direct message.

Duo & Trio Pilates

Want a more personalized experience with a partner or friends? Duo and Trio sessions offer individualized instruction in a small, private setting. Start with our 3-Session Duo or Trio Intro Offer for $145 per person.

To book: Email or call Leaside Pilates, fill out our contact form, or send us a direct message.

Group Reformer Classes

Best for: Beginners who are comfortable exercising in a group setting and anyone looking to build a consistent Pilates practice. Start with our 3-Class Group Intro Offer for $75.

New to Pilates? Book a Level 1 or All Levels class. Experienced? Choose Level 2 or Athletic Reformer.

To book: Log in to your profile directly through our website, or download the Momence app to easily book and manage your classes.

Good to Know

Studio policies

Arrival

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit so we can get you settled and answer any questions.

What to wear

Comfortable, fitted activewear you can move in. Grip socks are mandatory for all Pilates sessions for safety and hygiene. If you don't have a pair, grip socks are available for purchase at the studio.

Cancellations

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy for all group classes and private, duo, and trio sessions. Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your scheduled session so we can offer the spot to another client. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the session, as well as no-shows, will result in the session being deducted from your package or subscription.

Booking & payment

All classes, sessions, and packages are booked and paid through Momence. You can create an account, purchase, and manage your schedule online, or download the Momence app.
