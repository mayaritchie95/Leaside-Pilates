Good to Know

Studio policies

Arrival

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit so we can get you settled and answer any questions.

What to wear

Comfortable, fitted activewear you can move in. Grip socks are mandatory for all Pilates sessions for safety and hygiene. If you don't have a pair, grip socks are available for purchase at the studio.

Cancellations

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy for all group classes and private, duo and trio sessions. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are considered a late cancellation and the session credit will be forfeited.

Booking your sessions/classes

Group classes can be purchased and booked through our website or the Momence app. Private, Duo & Trio sessions are arranged directly with our studio.
